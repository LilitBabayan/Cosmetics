# Design context — Lumora Cosmetics

- **Audience**: people browsing a small clean-beauty label online — mostly on
  mobile, comparison-shopping skincare/makeup/fragrance, price-sensitive
  ($13–$68 range) but responsive to a warm, trustworthy brand voice.
- **Use cases**: browse the catalog by category, open a product for detail,
  add to a cart, adjust quantities, check out (demo only — no real payment).
- **Brand personality**: quietly confident clean beauty — warm, ingredient-
  honest, not clinical and not maximalist. Think a considered independent
  label, not a mass drugstore brand or a luxury flagship.
- **Physical scene**: someone scrolling this on their phone in soft daylight,
  deciding what to add to a real cart — calm, unhurried, tactile.
